# Truth gap, 2026-10-14: how 6 truth findings got through writing and QC

Divit's spot-check under ruling 20: B's reviews blocked 7 of the 9 Automation pages written on the r15 pipeline, and 6 of those blocks were truth findings. A-r-spot1 then found a seventh of the same kind. This note says, for each one, which stage should have caught it and why it didn't, and what closes the gap. Per the repo rule it quotes no FAQ question text and no source text: findings are named by page, field and pattern.

## Root cause (one line)

`spec.claims_used` and `spec.domain_sources` were lists kept for the whole page, and no check tied them to a sentence. QC asked only that:
- the lists exist (R15, C2);
- each listed id is approved (C2);
- each quote has 6+ words (R15);
- the writer's own `plan.claims_to_source` appears among the sources (P2).

C3 catches only a fixed brand list plus a fact verb, or a percentage. As a result:
- any sentence about Emergent passed if the page listed any claim id;
- any statement of fact about the world passed unless it named a listed brand next to a fact verb;
- the writer decided what needed a source.

## What closes it (QC C6, C7; `qc/truth.py`, `rules/truth.json`; wired into `qc/qc_hub.py`; page-pack lines in `ops/skeleton.py pack`)

- **C6 (Emergent claims, every copy field and FAQ answer).**
  - Emergent as the subject of a verb: only build-time verbs pass (builds, writes, changes, connects, exports, hosts, and the rest in `emergent_verbs`), or a sentence that matches an approved claim's wording. This includes verbs coordinated after the first verb, until a relative clause starts ("the app that ..., then ..." belongs to the app). Modal "can" is a buildable feature and passes.
  - free-start ends at its approved wording. A continuation that adds time, money or limit scope fails (`tail_markers`).
  - Billing, seat and credit terms in a sentence about Emergent must sit inside an approved wording.
  - A sentence that states a claim's wording must have that id in `claims_used`.
- **C7 (third-party facts, in FAQ answers, feature bodies and the features subheading).**
  - A flat Yes or No answer needs a quote that covers the answer's first two sentences. This applies when the question asks whether category software, a provider or a law does something, and is not an example, need, recommendation or same-as question. Hedged answers ("Yes, if/once ...") and app-voice answers pass.
  - A product-category subject with a hard qualifier (never, only, all, every, "N kinds", "come in") needs a quote that carries the qualifier. Advice ("should") and soft quantifiers ("some", "most") stay notes, per SEVERITY.
  - A sentence that names a vendor, law or regulator is bound to a quote. Binding means content-word overlap with the primary keyword's words removed; a vendor may also bind through its own source.
  - A definition of the primary that binds to a quote must keep its numbers and scope qualifiers inside the bound quotes. Synonyms such as must/required and annually/once a year count as matches, and so do numbers inside names ("Form 990"). Integration targets in the api-connect claim (Stripe, HubSpot, ...) and imperative advice pass.

## Scope: ruling 15

- New writing only. The rule runs on r15 pages whose skeleton was built at or after `rules/truth.json since` and that have no review.
- `hubctl recheck` cannot send a reviewed or live page to rework or to the fix queue because of it. Golden `truth-gate-*` cases check this, and today 0 specs are in scope.

Measured on every r15 spec, judged as new writing with no gating: 6 hits on 50 pages, every one in a class above.

**Same-class misses left as they are (ruling 15, no recheck).** Listed for Divit's call:

**Before precision tuning.** Before tuning, the rule also flagged sentences reviewers had accepted:
- volume consequences of no-caps ("a busy month runs the same");
- wording variants ("billed in credits", "tables you own", "puts no cap on form responses");
- vendors named as integration targets;
- imperative advice naming a form;
- numbers inside names;
- the topic word read as a brand on the SEO page.

Each now has a negative golden case.
