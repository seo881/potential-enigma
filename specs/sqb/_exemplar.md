## What Emergent Builds Into a Board Survey
Emergent builds the survey and the table behind it, and a follow-up prompt changes either one before the next round.

**Questions grouped by governance area**  
Name the areas your board wants to rate, such as fundraising and strategy. Emergent drafts a section for each, with rating and comment questions, and you edit any of them by prompt.

**Scores kept apart from names**  
The survey tracks who has answered so reminders stop, but saves each score without a name. The chair sees totals per area and every comment, never one director's ratings.

**Every answer in a database you own**  
Each response is a row in a database you own, tagged with the year and the area rated. Put this year's fundraising score beside last year's before the governance committee meets.

**Reminders until the deadline**  
Directors who have not answered get an email reminder 3 days before the deadline, and the board secretary gets a Slack list of who is still out. Anyone finished gets no more emails.

**One question per screen on a phone**  
Directors answer one question per screen on a phone, between meetings or on a train. Owners in a condo survey get the same flow, and Emergent puts no cap on how many answer.

## How Four Boards Run Their Surveys
**Tab 1: Self-Assessment**  
_Fundraising rated strong by 4 of 13 directors_  
Larkspur Food Bank sends its board self-assessment to all 14 directors each January, and no names are stored beside the scores. Of the 14, 13 answered. On mission, 12 of 13 rated the board strong. On fundraising, only 4 of 13 did, and the governance committee put fundraising first on the retreat agenda.

**Tab 2: Meeting Review**  
_Chair emailed when 3 directors call the packet late_  
Ostrander Library Foundation sends 3 questions to its 9 directors after every board meeting. When 3 or more say the packet arrived too late to read, the chair gets an email. After the May meeting, 5 of 9 said it. The chair moved the packet date to 7 days before each meeting.

**Tab 3: Condo Owners**  
_120 units asked how to pay for a $144,000 roof_  
Cobble Quay's condo board needs $144,000 for a new roof and asks owners how to pay. The survey takes one answer per unit, checked against the owner roster, and 87 of 120 units answered. Of those, 58 chose 2 installments of $600 over a single $1,200 payment. The board took that plan to the owners' meeting.

**Tab 4: Skills Matrix**  
_Technology, the skill none of 11 directors has_  
Two seats open on the Wrenfield Community Health board in March. First, all 11 directors rate their own experience in 6 areas. The matrix shows 6 directors with finance experience and only 1 with legal. Technology shows 0, and the nominating committee wrote it into the brief for both open seats.

## How to Run a Board Survey
Seven steps to run a board survey that directors answer frankly and that ends with a short list of agreed changes.

**01 Decide what the board is rating**  
Pick one scope per round: the whole board, or each director on their own. BoardSource recommends that boards assess their overall performance every two years, and a yearly round suits a board tracking one change. Put the scope in your prompt, and Emergent drafts the survey around it.

**02 Settle who reads the raw answers**  
Before launch, name the one person who reads raw comments, such as the governance chair, and agree that everyone else sees only the summary. Put that promise in the invitation in one sentence. Then check the build keeps it before a single director answers.

**03 Choose five or six areas to rate**  
Rate what the board itself controls, like strategy, financial oversight, fundraising, and recruiting new directors. Give each area three or four statements. Cap the survey near 20 statements, so a director can finish it in one sitting instead of saving it for a weekend.

**04 Pair each rating with a comment**  
Use the same 5-point agree scale for every statement, so areas compare within the survey and across years. Under each section, ask one open question: what would raise this score? A score shows where the board is weak, and the comment says what to change.

**05 Test it with two committee members**  
Before the invite goes out, have two governance committee members answer with their real views. Check that their scores land in the table without names and that each comment sits under the area it belongs to. Fix any statement either of them misread.

**06 Open it for 2 weeks before the retreat**  
Give directors 2 weeks to answer and close the survey at least a month before the retreat. The committee needs that month to read every comment and draft the agenda. Send the link from the board chair, a name every director already knows.

**07 Share the results and pick 3 changes**  
Show the full board each area's share and the comments, grouped by theme, with no names. Agree on 3 changes at most, give each one an owner, and rate the same areas next round to see whether the scores moved.

## Board Survey Questions, Answered
**What is a board survey?**  
A board survey is a questionnaire a board of directors uses to rate its own work or to hear from the people it answers to. In a self-assessment, each director scores the board on areas such as strategy and fundraising. A condo or nonprofit board can also send one to owners or members before a decision.

**What are some good questions to ask a board member survey?**  
Good questions name one behavior a director can judge. Strong board survey questions ask whether directors understand the finances well enough to vote on the budget, and whether meetings spend more time on strategy than on reports. Ask each director whether they know what the board expects of them in fundraising. Close with one open question on what to stop doing.
<sub>Source: ?: “What are some good questions to ask a board member survey?”</sub>

**What is a form board survey?**  
A form board survey is a construction survey, not a questionnaire. A licensed land surveyor plots where the foundation forms sit on the lot. Mesquite, Texas, requires a certified form board survey plot plan, which must be available to the inspector before the phase I inspection will be approved. It shares a word with a board survey or a brand awareness survey, and nothing more.
<sub>Source: ?: “What is a form board survey?”</sub>

**Are online board surveys legit?**  
Yes, when the link comes from an address your board already uses and the secretary or chair can confirm it was sent. Treat any board survey that asks for a password or bank details as a phishing attempt. A board that builds its survey on Emergent can run it on its own domain, which Emergent includes and bills in credits.

**Are US census board surveys mandatory?**  
Some are. Your response to the American Community Survey is required by law. The Current Population Survey, which the Census Bureau runs monthly for the Bureau of Labor Statistics, is voluntary. Neither one comes from a board of any kind.
<sub>Source: ?: “are us census board surveys mandatory”</sub>

**What is a forum board survey?**  
A forum board survey is a poll posted on an online message board, where members vote in a poll or answer in a thread. It suits quick questions to an active community, such as which meetup date works. When members leave the forum for good, a short exit survey asks why while the reason is still fresh.
<sub>Source: ?: “what is a forum board survey”</sub>

**What is a condo board survey?**  
A condo board survey is a questionnaire a condominium association's board sends to unit owners before it decides something, such as a special assessment or a pool rule. Treat the results as advice, and hold any binding vote the way your bylaws set out. On Emergent, the answers sit in a database the association owns, next to each unit number.
<sub>Source: ?: “what is condo board survey”</sub>

**What is the purpose of a form board survey?**  
Its purpose is to prove the foundation sits in the right place on the lot. The surveyor checks that the forms are not encroaching into any building lines or easements. Mesquite, Texas, uses the plot plan to confirm the house complies with the required setbacks in its subdivision.
