## What the Adjuster Needs, Captured at the Scene
Memory fades by the time someone fills in a paper form back at the office. These six pieces collect the facts at the roadside.

**Questions that follow the kind of accident**  
Choose a vehicle crash and the form asks for both plates and the police report number. Choose an injury instead and it asks which body part was hurt and where it was treated.

**Photos taken without leaving the form**  
Photograph the damage and the other vehicle from inside the report. Each photo is saved with the time it was taken, and nobody has to chase pictures by email a week later.

**The other driver's card, read into fields**  
Photograph the other driver's insurance card and license. The form reads the insurer and policy number into the report, and anything it cannot read goes back to the reporter.

**Every accident type reaches its owner**  
Send vehicle crashes to the fleet manager by email and injuries to HR in Slack. A report that mentions a hospital visit also alerts the safety lead the moment it is filed.

**Witness statements on the same phone**  
Hand the phone to a witness to type a statement and sign it. Their contact details and signature stay attached to the report that the adjuster reads when the claim opens.

**Code you can move to your own servers**  
On paid plans, export the full codebase to your GitHub repository when reports need to live inside an existing claims system. Host it anywhere and keep building on it.

## How to Create an Accident Report Form
Seven steps to create an accident report form that records the facts at the scene and puts each report in front of its owner.

**01 Start with the accidents you see most**  
Look back over the last year of accidents, such as van crashes or lifting injuries. Each one becomes a type on the form with its own questions. A plumbing company and a hotel end up with different lists.

**02 Ask for what an adjuster asks for**  
Collect when and where it happened, who was hurt and which vehicles were involved. Add a plain account of events in order. Keep fault and blame off the form, because the insurer and the investigation decide those.

**03 Make photos part of the report**  
Require photos for any vehicle crash or property damage. Add a step for the insurance card from the other car, which the form reads into fields. The reporter corrects anything that came through unclear before submitting.

**04 Pick who hears about each type**  
Decide who receives each kind of accident and how. Crashes might email the fleet manager while injuries post to an HR channel in Slack. Anything with a hospital visit can alert the safety lead too. Name a backup for nights and weekends.

**05 Describe the form to Emergent**  
Put the accident types and routing rules into one prompt. Emergent builds the accident report form, the database behind it, and a screen for whoever reviews reports. Ask in plain English when a question needs changing later.

**06 Match each report to its claim**  
Add fields for the insurance claim number and the date the claim closed. The risk team then sees which reports turned into claims and how long each one has been open. Claims that stall come back to someone by name.

**07 File a test crash**  
File a test crash from a phone in the parking lot using a made-up insurance card. Check that the email reaches the fleet manager and that the report lands in the table under the right type.

## Accident Report Questions, Answered
**What is an accident report form?**  
An accident report form is the written record of an accident at work or on the road. It records the time and place and who was involved. A factual account of what happened follows, with any injuries or damage. Insurers and HR teams use it to open claims.

**Can you provide an example of an accident report form?**  
Yes. Picture a delivery driver rear-ended at a stop sign. The form opens with the date, the time, and the intersection. The driver's name and van number sit below. The other car's plate and insurer go beside the police report number. Photos of both bumpers and a witness's phone number come last, before a few sentences on what happened, in order.

**What should an auto accident report form include?**  
Record each driver's license and insurer, along with the plates and the crash location. Add the police report number, photos of the damage, and witness contacts. A commercial vehicle accident report form also asks for the unit number and the load. Pair it with a vehicle inspection form to log the truck's condition before its next run.
<sub>Source: Secondary keyword: “auto accident report form”</sub>

**What is the difference between an injury report form and an accident report form?**  
Scope. An injury report form records one person who got hurt, down to the body part and the treatment. An accident report form covers more ground, including crashes and property damage where nobody was injured. One form can handle both, with injury questions that appear only when someone was hurt.
<sub>Source: Secondary keyword: “injury report form”</sub>

**Where can I find a free accident report form template?**  
Check with your insurer or employer first, and use their version if they have one. Universities and government agencies also post free copies, and form builders offer an accident report form template to start from. Emergent builds the same fields from a plain-English description, with no response caps.
<sub>Source: Secondary keyword: “accident report form template”</sub>

**How do I customize an accident report form template?**  
Cut every question that does not fit the accidents you handle. A vehicle accident report form needs plates and a police report number, while injury questions ask what hurts and who treated it. Show each section only when it applies. Then rename fields in the words reporters use and choose who gets each kind of report.
<sub>Source: Secondary keyword: “accident report form template”</sub>

**Which fields belong on an injury report form?**  
Start with who was hurt and the task under way when it happened. Add the date and place, then the body part and any first aid or clinic visit. Close with witness names and the supervisor who filed it. A motor vehicle accident report form keeps these questions and adds plates and insurers on top.
<sub>Source: Secondary keyword: “injury report form”</sub>

**Can an injury report form be used for near misses?**  
Not usually. An injury report form is built around a person who got hurt, and a near miss leaves no injury to record. Log near misses on an incident report form instead, with the hazard and the person who will fix it.
<sub>Source: Secondary keyword: “injury report form”</sub>

**Can you fill out an auto accident report form online?**  
Yes, and a phone at the scene is the best place to do it, while plates and witness names are easy to get. Photos go straight into the report instead of sitting in a camera roll. A car accident report form built with Emergent keeps every report in a database you own.
